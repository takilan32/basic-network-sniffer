Learn how to recognize and prevent phishing attacks.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations to steal sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, banking details, or OTPs.

Common Types of Phishing

  • Email Phishing
  • Spear Phishing
  • Whaling
  • Smishing (SMS)
  • Vishing (Voice Calls)

How to Identify Phishing Emails

  • Unknown sender
  • Spelling mistakes
  • Urgent language
  • Fake links
  • Unexpected attachments

How to Spot Fake Websites

  • Check HTTPS
  • Look for spelling errors in URL
  • Verify domain name
  • Avoid clicking pop-ups

Social Engineering Techniques

  • Pretexting
  • Baiting
  • Tailgating
  • Scareware
  • Impersonation

Best Practices

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication
  • Never share passwords
  • Verify suspicious emails
  • Keep software updated
  • Use strong passwords

Real World Example

Many attackers send fake bank emails asking users to verify their accounts by clicking malicious links.
